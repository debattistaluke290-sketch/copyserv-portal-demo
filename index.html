Good to know

Frequently asked questions

The answers the front desk gives a hundred times a day — now self-serve.

What file formats can I upload?
PDF works best — you'll see a live preview and an instant price. We also accept Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), Pages, and images (JPG/PNG); we convert them to print-ready PDF automatically. Max file size 100 MB.
How do you decide colour vs black & white?
Our system scans every page and only charges colour for pages that actually contain colour. You can override to all-B&W or all-colour at any time. B&W is €0.05/page, colour €0.25/page.
How long will my order take?
Most jobs are ready in 30–60 minutes. Large runs or hardback binding take a little longer. You'll always see a guaranteed "ready by" time before you pay, based on the shop's live workload.
Can you deliver, or do I collect?
Both. Collect free from the counter or a 24/7 smart locker, or get it delivered anywhere in Malta & Gozo for €4. You choose at checkout.
What binding and paper can I choose?
Binding: loose, stapled, comb, spiral or thermal hardback. Paper: 80gsm standard, 100gsm premium or 160gsm card. Add lamination or printed covers as options.
How do I pay?
Securely online by card, Revolut, Apple Pay or Google Pay — or choose pay at pickup. You get a receipt by email and WhatsApp.
Is my file private and secure?
Yes. Files are encrypted, used only to produce your order, and automatically deleted after collection. We never share them.
Do you handle bulk, student or school orders?
Absolutely — bulk discounts for large runs and monthly invoiced accounts for schools, colleges and tutors. Ask the AI desk to set one up.
